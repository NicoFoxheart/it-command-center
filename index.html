🎣 Phishing Teacher - Learn to Spot Scams
📧

Phishing attacks exploit trusted relationships to steal your credentials and personal data. Learn to identify red flags before you fall victim.

Amazon Account Verification
Medium
From: account-verify@amazon-security.com Subject: Your Amazon Account Needs Immediate Attention Dear Valued Customer, We've detected unusual activity on your account. Click below to verify your identity immediately or your account will be suspended. [VERIFY YOUR ACCOUNT] <-- SUSPICIOUS LINK

Red Flags 🚩

  • Generic greeting ("Dear Valued Customer")
  • Urgency & threat ("immediately" / "suspended")
  • Unusual sender domain (not @amazon.com)
  • Clickable link instead of directing to official site
  • No specific account details or order reference
PayPal Security Alert
Hard
From: billing@paypal.com Subject: Action Required: Payment Method Update Hi Sarah, Your payment method on file will expire soon. To continue uninterrupted service, update your billing info here. [UPDATE PAYMENT] <-- Looks official but... Questions? Reply to this email.

Red Flags 🚩

  • Urgent action needed language
  • Generic greeting (should use your name)
  • Official-looking domain (could be spoofed)
  • Asks you to click link vs. directing to official site
  • Creates artificial scarcity ("expire soon")
SMS Phishing (Smishing)
Easy
From: +1 (234) 567-8901 Hi! UPS tried to deliver your package. Confirm your address here: short-url-link.com/upstracking

Red Flags 🚩

  • You weren't expecting a package
  • Shortened URL (hides real destination)
  • Request for personal info via text
  • Official companies don't text you links like this
  • Unusual phone number format
    • Bank Account Locked
    Medium
    From: security@yourbank.net Subject: URGENT: Account Locked Your account has been locked due to suspicious login attempts. Verify your identity with SSN, DOB, and mother's maiden name: [UNLOCK ACCOUNT NOW]

    Red Flags 🚩

  • Real banks never ask for SSN or full DOB via email
  • ALL CAPS urgency language
  • Suspicious sender domain (.net, not official bank domain)
  • Requests sensitive personal data
  • Artificial urgency ("URGENT")
