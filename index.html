🎣 Phishing Teacher — Learn to Spot Scams

Phishing attacks exploit trusted relationships to steal your credentials and personal data. Each example below is styled to look like the real thing — study them, then take the quiz.

Amazon Account Verification
Medium
From: account-verify@amazon-security.com
To: you@email.com
⚠️ Your Amazon Account Needs Immediate Attention
Dear Valued Customer,

We've detected unusual activity on your account. Click below to verify your identity immediately or your account will be suspended within 24 hours.

⚠️ Suspicious link hidden here Verify Your Account →

Red Flags 🚩

  • Generic greeting — Amazon always uses your real name
  • Fake sender domain: amazon-security.com ≠ amazon.com
  • Urgency + threat ("immediately or suspended")
  • No specific order or account reference
  • Button hides the real destination URL
PayPal Security Alert
Hard
From: billing@paypal.com ← spoofed — hover to see real sender
To: you@email.com
Action Required: Payment Method Update
Hi Sarah,

Your payment method on file will expire soon. To continue uninterrupted service, please update your billing information below.

⚠️ Looks legit — it's not Update Payment →

Questions? Reply to this email.

Red Flags 🚩

  • Domain looks official but the header is spoofed — check the full "From" address
  • Uses someone else's name ("Sarah") — wrong recipient
  • Artificial urgency: "expire soon" without a date
  • Directs you to click rather than navigate to paypal.com yourself
  • "Reply to this email" — legitimate billing never resolves that way
SMS Phishing (Smishing)
Easy
+1 (234) 567-8901
Hi! UPS tried to deliver your package. Confirm your address here: short-url-link.com/upstracking

Red Flags 🚩

  • Random phone number — UPS sends from known short codes
  • Shortened URL hides the real destination
  • No tracking number, no sender verification
  • Asks you to confirm personal data via link
  • Unexpected package you weren't tracking
Bank Account Locked
Medium
From: security@yourbank.net
To: you@email.com
URGENT: Account Locked — Action Required
Your account has been locked due to suspicious login attempts.

Verify your identity with your SSN, date of birth, and mother's maiden name:

⚠️ Never give this info via email Unlock Account Now →

Red Flags 🚩

  • Real banks never ask for SSN or full DOB via email
  • ALL-CAPS urgency is a manipulation tactic
  • Sender domain: .net not your bank's real domain
  • Requests your mother's maiden name — a security question answer
  • No branch name, no customer service number
AI Voice & Text Scam — The New Threat in 2026
⚡ New Attack
Mom 📱
Hey it's me, I lost my phone and I'm borrowing a friend's. I need $300 for an emergency — can you Venmo me at @not-moms-venmo? Don't call this number, it won't work. Love you ❤️

Scammers now use AI to clone voices and writing styles from social media. They know your mom's name, how she texts, even her sign-off.

Red Flags 🚩

  • Unfamiliar number claiming to be someone you know
  • "Don't call" — they don't want you to verify
  • Urgent money request via an app with no way to reverse
  • AI can perfectly clone a loved one's voice or text style
  • Always verify via a second channel before sending money
  • Create a family safe-word only real family would know
